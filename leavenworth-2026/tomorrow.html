One long day to Wenatchee Confluence

You are still home in Boise. The sensible first day is one tow, all the way to Wenatchee Confluence State Park. There is no comfortable overnight on the way that is known to fit the F-350 and the X195.
I-84 west, I-82 through Yakima, US-97 over Blewett Pass, US-2 into Wenatchee. The map trace is about 445 miles. Plan 8.5–10 hours door to door with fuel and food. A car is faster; the map’s driving time is the car time. Blewett is the snow risk. From Nov 1 to Apr 1 the rig has to carry chains on that route, and Ford bans chains on the Tremor’s LT285/75R18 tires. Solve that before a storm day. Wenatchee sunset in December is about 4:10 p.m. Leave early enough to take a first-come site in daylight.

Intended base Wenatchee Confluence State Park

47.4605, -120.3272
First night if you drive through. Loop 2, first-come Oct 16–Mar 31. Winter full-utility $41. Water off around Nov 1; frost-free fill near sites 34 and 36. Max 65 ft. About 30–40 minutes to Leavenworth the next day, no pass. Suggested stay 3–5 midweek nights, not reserved. Call 509-664-6373 if you want to confirm electric before you roll.
