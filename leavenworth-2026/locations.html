Read before you go

  • Chains, before anything else. From Nov 1 to Apr 1, a vehicle or combination over 10,000 lb GVWR must carry enough tire chains for WAC 204-24-050 on I-82 Ellensburg–Selah, US-97 from milepost 145 to the US-2 junction (Blewett), and US-2 from Dryden (milepost 108) to Index (milepost 36). Leavenworth is on that US-2 stretch, so the day trip west of Dryden is inside the carry zone, truck included. Also carry two extra chains. Approved chains for these rigs are metal; the rule says plastic chains are not allowed. When “chains required” is posted, a two-to-four-axle combination (pickup plus tandem trailer) chains one tire on each side of a drive axle and one tire on the last trailer axle. Ford bans snow chains on LT285/75R18, the Tremor’s stock size. Do not start this drive in posted chain conditions until the traction plan is legal for both Washington and the truck. Pass report: WSDOT mountain passes.
  • Snow socks are not a blanket yes. WAC 204-24-050(3) counts a device as “chained” only if it is on the Washington State Patrol list for that vehicle. The Patrol chart lists AutoSock as approved for combinations over 10,000 lb with five or fewer axles, and not approved for six or more axles. Other sock brands on that chart (Grip Tex, SOS Grip, Easy Grip EVO, Polaire) are not approved over 10,000 lb. Carrying AutoSock does not, by itself, meet the written carry-chains rule, and Ford’s position on a fabric device on LT285/75R18 is not confirmed here.
  • No overnight RV on Leavenworth city streets. Municipal Code 10.14. Fine is $500/day. Day-use parking only. Leave the trailer in camp. Truck parking for town is Willkommen Park & Ride, 200 Ward Strasse behind Safeway, then the free Link shuttle. City RV parking sheet · code · lots.
  • No black tank. Do not plan or hunt a dump station. Carry onboard water and fill at a frost-free valve.
  • Lifetime Senior Pass does not discount Washington state parks or private RV parks. It is federal only. A Discover Pass is not required while you are camped in that state park.
  • X195 cold. The trailer is Imperial-rated to about −40°F (insulated, heated tanks, Truma Combi) and does not need winterizing. It is still unconfirmed whether the gray drain and macerator sit inside the heated area. Freeze risk remains if the heat is off, or on a very cold night. December days are short, so propane and heat demand go up.
  • Daylight. In Wenatchee, timeanddate lists about 8 hours 25–40 minutes of daylight through December 2026, with sunset around 4:10 p.m. Confluence is first-come in winter. Arrive with enough light to take a site. Do not plan to hunt sites after dark.
  • Snow is on the tow, not the day trip. The lower valley (about 600–1,200 ft; Confluence about 640 ft) is milder than Lake Wenatchee, Stevens Pass, or Plain. Confluence to Leavenworth is US-2 with no mountain pass. Blewett Pass (about 4,102 ft) is the snow and access risk, and that is on the drive from Boise with the trailer.
  • Water. Confluence water off around Nov 1 (frost-free fill). KOA keeps water on. Blu Shastin: call. Thousand Trails water and sewer off Oct–Apr.
  • Suggested stay: 3–5 midweek nights. Leave the trailer in camp. Take the truck, or the free Link shuttle from Willkommen, for evening lights. Midweek is much easier than Saturday, when parking fills by midday and town manages traffic about noon–8 p.m.
  • No Harvest Hosts pin. A public page, without a membership login, did not confirm a Harvest Hosts overnight near Wenatchee. Chelan County Expo Center in Cashmere and Wenatchee River County Park are April–October. Wenatchee Elks #1186 is gone (disbanded 2006).

Candidate sits

Intended base Wenatchee Confluence State Park

Best winter value. Washington State Parks, 333 Olds Station Rd, Wenatchee, WA 98801. Loop 2 is open year-round. Camping is first-come, first-served Oct 16–Mar 31. Winter rates: full-utility $41, partial $35, standard $23. 52 full-hookup sites when water is on, plus 8 standard. Max length 65 ft (limited). Water turns off around Nov 1; frost-free fill is near sites 34 and 36 (Loop 2 restroom). Restrooms stay open. Electric is the usual pattern on full-utility sites and still worth confirming on arrival, because the pedestal water may be off — park phone 509-664-6373. About 21 miles / 30–40 minutes to downtown Leavenworth on US-2, no pass. Elevation about 640 ft. Non-resident reservation surcharge does not apply to winter first-come camping.

In town Leavenworth / Pine Village KOA Holiday

The “sleep in the Christmas lights” option. Ed usually prefers not to use a KOA, so this is not the default. 308 Zelt Strasse, Leavenworth, WA 98826. Open all year, with water still on, and big-rig pull-throughs commonly listed to 65 ft. About 1.5–2 miles / 5–10 minutes to Front Street, and walkable to the Willkommen park-and-ride. Published rates have been roughly $80–$120+ a night before taxes; Christmas weekends run higher and sell out. Call 509-548-7709. The park gets feet of snow. They plow; expect ice. Pets about $3 a night on RV sites.

Mid-valley Blu Shastin RV Park, Peshastin

Between Wenatchee and town, on the US-97 arrival corridor. 3300 US Hwy 97, Peshastin, WA 98847. Listed at $60 a night full hookup, sites to about 60 ft. Directory listings say year-round with limited winter facilities. Call 888-548-4184 and confirm December electric and water before you count on the spigot. About 12 miles / 20–25 minutes to Leavenworth on US-2.

Members Thousand Trails Leavenworth

Only if you already have a Northwest membership and want snow. 20752 Chiwawa Loop Rd, near Plain / Lake Wenatchee. Open year-round, limited sites Nov 1–Apr 30. Water and sewer unavailable Oct–Apr; fill at Osprey Lodge. About 17 miles / 30–35 minutes to town via WA-207 and US-2. Higher and snowier than the valley. The park warns that RVs over 30 ft may not fit some snowy sites. The X195 is about 26 ft, so length is less of the issue than snow and membership. Pin is the park center published with the Thousand Trails listing, not a single site.

Key stop Leavenworth Christmastown / Village of Lights

Front Street Park and the gazebo, 820 Front Street. Christmastown festivities run Nov 27–Dec 24, 2026 (Black Friday through Christmas Eve). Village of Lights is on daily 6 a.m.–11 p.m. from Thanksgiving (Nov 26) through Feb 28, 2027. Lights, gazebo music and characters, and the daytime Festhalle warming station are free. Separate Festhalle concerts and movies are door-only tickets and are not required for the lights. There is no separate “North Pole Express” event to plan on in Leavenworth.

Park the truck at Willkommen Park & Ride, 200 Ward Strasse (behind Safeway), and ride the free Link shuttle. The shuttle has been described as about 11 a.m. to 7 p.m.; confirm hours the week you go. City lots are day-use. No overnight, and do not take the trailer into town.
