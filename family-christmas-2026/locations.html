Per-area notes (kids, hot springs, tubing, XC, indoor, groceries, RV, drive times, rentals) are on details.html. 7 people indoors (6 adults/teens plus Emily, 2). Ed and Vicki stay in the X195. Prices are Airbnb search-card totals for 7 guests in USD, pulled on Nov 10. They include Airbnb fees, but taxes may not be included, and none has been checked at checkout. Ranking (snow reliability, hot springs, affordable variety, rental fit, RV)

  1. Long Valley: McCall / Donnelly / Cascade (top pick).
  2. Snow is reliable at 4,800–5,000 ft.
  3. Two family hot springs within 15–35 min:
    • Gold Fork has a sandy-bottom kids' pool. In winter, kids under 13 must leave by 4 pm, Thursdays are 18+, and it is closed Tuesdays.
    • Zims has a 90–100°F swim pool. It is closed Mon/Tue and Christmas Day.
  4. Activity Barn tubing: $30, riders must be 36"+ tall.
  5. Free sledding at Legacy Park.
  6. Lots of groomed Nordic for Vicki: Ponderosa 19 km, Jug Mountain 25 km at $17, Bear Basin 30 km, and a free 5 km Activity Barn loop.
  7. Little Ski Hill and Brundage for cheap skiing.
  8. It is the shortest and easiest tow for Ed (~2.3–2.6 h) and 3 h for Joel's family.
  9. Two of the rentals allow RV parking on site.
  10. Lava Hot Springs (+ Pocatello).
  11. Best hot springs: open daily except Christmas Day, closing at 6 pm Christmas Eve. The indoor Kiddie Cove suits Emily, and the town is walkable.
  12. Cheapest king-heavy houses.
  13. Easiest interstate tow (~4.4 h).
  14. Snow in town (~5,000 ft) is less reliable. Tubing and skiing are at Pebble Creek (~45 min), and Nordic is at Mink Creek (south of Pocatello).
  15. RV: the KOA has only 3 winter sites.
  16. Island Park / Ashton.
  17. Deepest, surest snow (4–7 ft on the ground at peak, per a host).
  18. Harriman SP has 33+ mi of groomed Nordic, which is best for Vicki.
  19. Henrys Lake SP has plowed winter dry RV sites.
  20. One house has its own RV hookup.
  21. Downsides: hot springs are 80–95 min away (Green Canyon, Heise), and it is the longest drive (~5.3–6 h).
  22. Teton Valley / Driggs.
  23. Great snow and Grand Targhee.
  24. Green Canyon hot springs is ~40 min away, but it is closed Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and Sundays.
  25. Teton Valley RV in Victor has winter full hookups.
  26. About 6 h from Boise via Idaho Falls/ID-33. No Teton Pass needed.
  27. Stanley.
  28. Most dramatic, with certain snow, a free ice rink and Mountain Village hot springs.
  29. Downsides: extreme cold, ID-21 avalanche closures (the hardest tow), thin services, and queen-only or pricey rentals.

Dropped or not pursued: - Sun Valley and Park City: excluded by Ed. - Bend/Sunriver: no developed hot springs nearby. - Baker City/La Grande: Anthony Lakes is good for Nordic, but there is no winter family hot-springs pool verified nearby. Not researched in depth. - Jarbidge: no rentals, and winter road access is poor. - Elko/Ruby and Logan/Bear Lake: not researched this pass.

Near Park City (Porter's work in Park City — cheaper bases nearby)

Park City itself is excluded as pricey. Prices are Airbnb search-card totals for Dec 21–28, 7 guests (taxes possibly excluded, not checked at checkout).

Ranking

  1. Heber City / Midway (best all-round). ~25 min to Park City. It has:
  2. Homestead Crater (90–96°F, $30/hr session);
  3. Soldier Hollow tubing (opens Dec 18) and Olympic Nordic ($24 adult day pass, snowmaking loop, $30/day Chariot child trailer for Emily);
  4. Wasatch Mountain SP full hookups ($50/night, winter loop open);
  5. Mountain Valley RV (year-round);
  6. North Pole Express train;
  7. real grocery stores.

Downsides: rentals run $2.9–5.5k, and it is the longest tow for Ed (6.3 h). 2. Ogden Valley (Eden). 1.5 h to Park City, 5.2 h from Boise. It has: - Ogden Nordic (25 km; $20 adult, under 11 free); - Wasatch Parc tubing (from $33); - Crystal Hot Springs ~1 h away ($18, age 3 and under free); - Treehouse Museum ($12 age 2+).

Downside: no verified winter RV park in the valley. 3. Logan / Cache Valley + Bear Lake (cheapest). Rentals run $1.7–2.4k, and Logan is 2.2 h to Park City. Crystal Hot Springs is ~35 min from Logan. Sun Outdoors Garden City is a year-round RV lead. Nordic, tubing and sledding are thinly verified. 4. Kamas / Oakley. 20 min to Park City and close to Homestead, but the only 4-BR fit was $4,154. Treat it as overflow for Heber. 5. Elko / Spring Creek NV. Halfway between Boise and Park City, but it has no verified family hot springs, Nordic or tubing, and the Elks lodge has no RV parking. Use it only as a meet-in-the-middle stop. - Dropped: Evanston WY (no hot springs, few rentals) and Coalville (cabins $5–7k).

Versus Stanley and McCall

  • McCall/Long Valley is still the best Boise-centered pick:
  • only 2.3 h from Boise;
  • two kid-friendly hot springs;
  • lots of Nordic and an ice rink;
  • RV allowed at two rentals.

But it is 8.4 h from Park City, so it does not work for Porter's work. - Stanley is a candidate only; Porter previewed Beckwith's, nothing is booked. - Price: $3,699 for Dec 21–28, all queens. - Snow is near-certain, but it has the hardest tow (ID-21), extreme cold and thin services. - It is 7.3 h from Park City. - Heber gives similar winter variety to McCall, with better tubing and Nordic infrastructure and a warmer, toddler-capable crater soak, 25 min from Porter's work. The cost is +4 h towing for Ed and somewhat higher rents.
