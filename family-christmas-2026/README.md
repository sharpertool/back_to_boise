## Ranking (snow reliability, hot springs, affordable variety, rental fit, RV)
1. **Long Valley: McCall / Donnelly / Cascade (top pick).**
   - Snow is reliable at 4,800–5,000 ft.
   - Two family hot springs within 15–35 min:
     - Gold Fork has a sandy-bottom kids' pool. In winter, kids under 13 must leave by 4 pm, Thursdays are 18+, and it is closed Tuesdays.
     - Zims has a 90–100°F swim pool. It is closed Mon/Tue and Christmas Day.
   - Activity Barn tubing: $30, riders must be 36"+ tall.
   - Free sledding at Legacy Park.
   - Lots of groomed Nordic for Vicki: Ponderosa 19 km, Jug Mountain 25 km at $17, Bear Basin 30 km, and a free 5 km Activity Barn loop.
   - Little Ski Hill and Brundage for cheap skiing.
   - It is the shortest and easiest tow for Ed (~2.3–2.6 h) and 3 h for Joel's family.
   - Two of the rentals allow RV parking on site.
2. **Lava Hot Springs (+ Pocatello).**
   - Best hot springs: open daily except Christmas Day, closing at 6 pm Christmas Eve. The indoor Kiddie Cove suits Emily, and the town is walkable.
   - Cheapest king-heavy houses.
   - Easiest interstate tow (~4.4 h).
   - Snow in town (~5,000 ft) is less reliable. Tubing and skiing are at Pebble Creek (~45 min), and Nordic is at Mink Creek (south of Pocatello).
   - RV: the KOA has only 3 winter sites.
3. **Island Park / Ashton.**
   - Deepest, surest snow (4–7 ft on the ground at peak, per a host).
   - Harriman SP has 33+ mi of groomed Nordic, which is best for Vicki.
   - Henrys Lake SP has plowed winter dry RV sites.
   - One house has its own RV hookup.
   - Downsides: hot springs are 80–95 min away (Green Canyon, Heise), and it is the longest drive (~5.3–6 h).
4. **Teton Valley / Driggs.**
   - Great snow and Grand Targhee.
   - Green Canyon hot springs is ~40 min away, but it is closed Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and Sundays.
   - Teton Valley RV in Victor has winter full hookups.
   - About 6 h from Boise via Idaho Falls/ID-33. No Teton Pass needed.
5. **Stanley.**
   - Most dramatic, with certain snow, a free ice rink and Mountain Village hot springs.
   - Downsides: extreme cold, ID-21 avalanche closures (the hardest tow), thin services, and queen-only or pricey rentals.

Dropped or not pursued:
- Sun Valley and Park City: excluded by Ed.
- Bend/Sunriver: no developed hot springs nearby.
- Baker City/La Grande: Anthony Lakes is good for Nordic, but there is no winter family hot-springs pool verified nearby. **Not researched in depth.**
- Jarbidge: no rentals, and winter road access is poor.
- Elko/Ruby and Logan/Bear Lake: **not researched this pass.**

## Near Park City (Porter's work in Park City — cheaper bases nearby)
Park City itself is excluded as pricey. Prices are Airbnb search-card totals for **Dec 21–28**, 7 guests (taxes possibly excluded, not checked at checkout).

### Ranking
1. **Heber City / Midway (best all-round).** ~25 min to Park City. It has:
   - Homestead Crater (90–96°F, $30/hr session);
   - Soldier Hollow tubing (opens Dec 18) and Olympic Nordic ($24 adult day pass, snowmaking loop, $30/day Chariot child trailer for Emily);
   - Wasatch Mountain SP full hookups ($50/night, winter loop open);
   - Mountain Valley RV (year-round);
   - North Pole Express train;
   - real grocery stores.

   Downsides: rentals run $2.9–5.5k, and it is the longest tow for Ed (6.3 h).
2. **Ogden Valley (Eden).** 1.5 h to Park City, 5.2 h from Boise. It has:
   - Ogden Nordic (25 km; $20 adult, under 11 free);
   - Wasatch Parc tubing (from $33);
   - Crystal Hot Springs ~1 h away ($18, age 3 and under free);
   - Treehouse Museum ($12 age 2+).

   Downside: no verified winter RV park in the valley.
3. **Logan / Cache Valley + Bear Lake (cheapest).** Rentals run $1.7–2.4k, and Logan is 2.2 h to Park City. Crystal Hot Springs is ~35 min from Logan. Sun Outdoors Garden City is a year-round RV lead. Nordic, tubing and sledding are thinly verified.
4. **Kamas / Oakley.** 20 min to Park City and close to Homestead, but the only 4-BR fit was $4,154. Treat it as overflow for Heber.
5. **Elko / Spring Creek NV.** Halfway between Boise and Park City, but it has no verified family hot springs, Nordic or tubing, and the Elks lodge has no RV parking. Use it only as a meet-in-the-middle stop.
- Dropped: Evanston WY (no hot springs, few rentals) and Coalville (cabins $5–7k).
