Echo, or drive through to Mountain Valley

You are still home in Boise. The first night out is a choice: stop at Echo State Park on I-80, or drive through and take the intended base at Mountain Valley RV Resort in Heber.
I-84 east to Echo Canyon, then US-40 south into Heber Valley. About 375 miles and 6.5–7 hours towing if you go all the way. The car route via Echo is about 394 miles. No black tank, so do not plan a stop to empty one. Utah state parks are full price; the Senior Pass does not discount them.
