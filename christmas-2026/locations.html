Read before you book

  • No black tank. Do not plan a stop to empty one. What matters at a site is electric and water.
  • Lifetime Senior Pass is 50% off federal campgrounds only. Utah state parks (Wasatch, Deer Creek, Echo, and the closed Jordanelle and Rockport campgrounds) are full price.
  • Utah traction law can apply on I-80 and US-40 when it is posted (Parleys, Mayflower, Daniels Canyon, SR-224). A 4x4 with M+S or 3PMSF tires complies while towing. Trailer chains are not required.
  • No Elks RV parking in Park City or Heber. Nearest is Provo Elks Lodge #849, about an hour away.
  • Heber Valley sits about 1,400 ft lower than Park City. December snow is about 18 inches in the valley and about 30 inches in Park City. December averages about 37°F days and 14°F nights in the valley. Park City is about 20 minutes up US-40.
  • Dispersed Forest Service camping is not realistic at Christmas. Roads are snowed in or groomed for snowmobiles.

Inbound / outbound Echo State Park

Optional one night on I-80, on the way in or out. First-come, first-served October 1–April 30. Utah State Parks fee table: partial hookup $40, full hookup $50. Water is off in winter; electric stays on. Vault toilets stay open; the main restroom and showers close. Senior Pass does not apply.

Key stop Park City town

Day trip, about 20 minutes up US-40 from the Heber Valley. Do not take the trailer into town. Park at a free Park City Transit park-and-ride and ride the free bus. Richardson Flat is the large lot (about 650 spaces) with the Old Town Express into Main Street. Winter hours change; check the city park-and-ride page the week you go.
