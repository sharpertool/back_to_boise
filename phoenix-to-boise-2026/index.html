Phoenix to Boise — Nov 28 to Dec 4

Locations →Details →
3-day plan

Leave Saturday Nov 28. Home by Friday Dec 4. Three drive days, ideally home Tuesday Dec 1 ahead of the Dec 2 rain. F-350 and the Imperial X195, about 26 ft. No KOAs.

Day 1. Phoenix area to Page Elks, or Hurricane Cliffs BLM. About 8–8.5 h via Page.

Day 2. If night 1 was Page, go to Dixie Elks in St. George. Otherwise continue north on I-15 toward Yuba or Jerome.

Day 3. Jerome Elks or Mountain Home Elks, then Boise.

Avoid UT-20, Cedar Breaks, US-89A over Jacob Lake in a storm, and the Zion tunnel. Angel Lake is closed. Times are OSRM car estimates, not Google and not tow times. Pins are search areas, not exact campsites. The start pin is the Greenway Road and I-17 interchange, not a house.
