Phoenix to Boise — stops

Nov 28–Dec 4, 2026. Every pin is an area center you can drop into onX. None of them is an exact boondock pad, and none is a house. No KOAs.

Suggested 3-day plan

Leave Saturday Nov 28, 2026. Be home by Friday Dec 4. Plan three drive days, and get home by Tuesday Dec 1 if you can, ahead of the Dec 2 rain. The rig is a 2025 F-350 and an Imperial X195 about 26 ft. No KOAs anywhere.

  1. Day 1. Phoenix area to Page Elks, or Hurricane Cliffs BLM. About 8–8.5 hours via Page.
  2. Day 2. If night 1 was Page, go to Dixie Elks in St. George. Otherwise continue north on I-15 toward the Yuba or Jerome area.
  3. Day 3. Jerome Elks or Mountain Home Elks, then Boise.

Winter cautions: avoid UT-20, Cedar Breaks, US-89A over Jacob Lake in a storm, and the Zion tunnel. Angel Lake is closed. Times are OSRM car estimates, not Google and not tow times. Pins are search areas or lodge/park centers, not exact boondock pads. The start pin is the public Greenway Road and I-17 interchange, not a house.
