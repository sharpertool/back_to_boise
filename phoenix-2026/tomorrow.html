Elko area, on the US-93 side

You are still home in Boise. The first night search is the Elko area, reached on the western corridor: I-84 east to Twin Falls, US-93 south through Jackpot to Wells, then I-80 west into Elko. A car router measures that at about 300 miles and a little over 5 hours. Plan a full day once you add 2–3 hours to hitch and the fuel stops. Do it on a weekend. A 3 p.m. weekday departure does not leave enough November light.
The “about 400 miles” planning guess is longer than this trace. Towns along US-93 and I-80 are the drive, not a dirt shortcut. Elko nights in November and December are often single digits or teens. The X195 is rated for deep cold with the heat on; a night with the heat off is still a freeze risk. No black tank, so no dump stop. The lifetime Senior Pass does not discount Nevada state parks. Arrive with light left to scout. Nothing below is reserved.

First look South Fork State Recreation Area

40.65720, -115.73886
South of Elko on SR-228, with the Rubies behind the reservoir. Nevada State Parks: camping open all year, 14 days in a 30-day period. East Campground has power sites; you pay the power fee if you take the site. Water spigots are fill-then-unhook. The Nov 1, 2026 state winterization list did not name this park. Call 775-744-4346 before you count on water. Non-Nevada vehicles pay the higher rate. Pin is the coordinate on the State Parks map link, a search area, not a site number.

Farther north Wild Horse State Recreation Area

41.66939, -115.80029
North of Elko on SR-225, on the reservoir. Open year-round, 14 days in 30. The park page does not list hookups, and a May 12, 2026 note said Loop 2 electricity was not in service yet. Water faucets are summer only. The Nov 1, 2026 winterization notice closed the flush restrooms and showers; pit toilets stay open. Colder than Elko. Pin is the State Parks map coordinate for the reservoir park, not an individual pad.

Do not count on it in November Lamoille Canyon

40.6472, -115.4068
The scenic Ruby Mountains drive, about 30 miles from Elko. The pin is Thomas Canyon Campground on the Forest Service page (7,200 ft). That campground’s reservation season is generally Memorial Day to Labor Day, and the canyon road is not plowed in winter. Scout it on a later trip, or on a day the road is actually open. It is not the first-night plan.
